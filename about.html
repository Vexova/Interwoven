About Interwoven

Our Story

Our Story

Interwoven began with a simple belief: every student deserves the opportunity to discover their potential through STEAM. We saw how differences in access to resources, mentors, and hands-on experiences could shape a student's future, and wanted to help close that gap.

Our Mission

At Interwoven, our mission is to make hands-on STEAM education accessible, inclusive, and inspiring for every student. Through workshops, mentorship, community partnerships, and student-led leadership, we empower young people to develop the skills and confidence to become the next generation of innovators, problem-solvers, and changemakers. We believe that every child, regardless of their background, gender, abilities, or circumstances, deserves the opportunity to explore STEAM in an engaging and supportive environment.

Our Vision

Where we're headed

We envision a world where every student has access to quality STEAM education and the opportunity to reach their full potential. Through student-led chapters, Interwoven makes a global impact, bringing meaningful STEAM experiences to underserved communities and students of all backgrounds, abilities, and circumstances.
