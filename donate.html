Every thread starts with someone willing to hold it.

Interwoven runs on volunteers, small grants, and gifts from people who believe STEAM shouldn't be a privilege. Your donation is what turns a chapter idea into an actual workshop, with actual materials, for actual students.

What your gift supports

Robotics kits, circuit boards, art supplies, printing, venue costs, and the small logistics that make a free workshop possible for a chapter that's often run entirely by volunteers.

Why it matters

Every chapter on our map — from Tanta to Toronto to Santa Clara — started because someone decided access to STEAM shouldn't be optional. Your donation is what lets that decision reach the next student.

How donations create impact

100% volunteer-run chapters mean your gift goes directly to programming, not overhead. Every dollar is one fewer barrier between a student and a hands-on STEAM experience.
